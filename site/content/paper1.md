## The claim in one paragraph

Given a definition of control as directed change relative to a held-fixed evaluative
structure, agency-preserving *conversion* — the transformation of that structure —
cannot be formulated as control without collapsing into one of three failures. The
argument is transcendental, not constructive: it proposes no new mechanism of
transformation, and instead argues that the inherited apparatus of cybernetics and
optimal control cannot so much as pose the question without annihilating the agency
of the subject it claims to transform.

## The trilemma

Three jointly exhaustive failure modes for any control-theoretic account of
conversion.

**Endogenous tautology.** Change generated by internal rules alone — Ashby's
requisite variety and ultrastability — is the execution of a latent subroutine. The
system changes *state* precisely so as not to change *organization*. Nothing has been
converted.

**Exogenous annihilation.** Coercive trajectory-tracking drives the agency
eigenvalues to negative infinity and zeroes adaptive diffusion: in the
perfect-tracking limit the controller must kill the agent to hold it on a prescribed
path. Finite-gain forcing pays a graded, monotone agency cost instead — which is a
weaker claim than the limit, and the suite measures the curve rather than asserting
the bifurcation.

**Incommensurability.** Optimization collapses at axiological discontinuities where
the cost function is itself replaced, producing singularities and indeterminacy in
backward induction.

The space these three leave empty is exactly the space a non-coercive transformation
would have to occupy. **Class G** is the residual admissibility profile that occupies
it — a conjunction of ten conditions, distinguished categorically from a unilateral
integrity-bounded transition by divergent post-jump topology rather than by
adjectival shielding.

## What the suite established, and what it cost

The load-bearing formal result is a **Meta-Optimization Collapse Theorem**, a
trichotomy. Its high-dimensional stress test (`high_dim_trichotomy`) reported its
strong-recession cells as *numerically unresolved* rather than claiming them either
way — and said so in the paper. The follow-up (`value_base_discontinuity_probe`) then
showed the apparent falsifier was a coordinate artifact. That result helped the
thesis, and it was only credible because the earlier run had declined to guess.

The existential risk to the whole positive residue was Class G's **satisfiability**.
A ten-condition conjunction invites two opposite and equally fatal objections: empty
by over-specification, or vacuous by under-specification. An explicit stochastic
witness meets all ten simultaneously against thresholds fixed in advance, and every
near-miss in the paper's own filter table is excluded, each failing a condition the
table predicts (`class_g_coherence`). Two instrument defects were found by a
self-test *before* the result was read; the thresholds were not loosened, the
numerics were tightened, and the fixes made discrimination stronger.

A follow-up then measured how much weight the count of ten can bear
(`class_g_independence`). Six of the ten break in isolation; the rest are entailed by
others, two of them as a matter of logic rather than of this particular construction.
The filter's effective dimensionality is about six, and the paper now says so rather
than leaving it for a reviewer.

## Scope

None of this is evidence that anything instantiates Class G. Satisfiability is a
logical property of a conjunction; whether any biological, psychological or
theological process meets the profile is exactly the question the paper declines to
answer, and it remains open.
