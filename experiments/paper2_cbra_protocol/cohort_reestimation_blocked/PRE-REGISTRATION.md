# H3 — re-test the B1 boundary residual on a cleaner cohort (BLOCKED, not run)

**Status: pre-registered but NOT EXECUTABLE — documented rather than run.**

## Hypothesis (from the research plan)
B1's pre-registered negative (interoceptive boundary residual beats a linear-Gaussian null in
only 6/21 = 29% of I-CARE patients, below the 60% bar) may be confounded by the cohort:
post-arrest patients under sedation/TTM/pressors have suppressed cardiac time-irreversibility (a
biomarker established to degrade with age and disease — Costa, Goldberger & Peng, PRL 95, 198102,
2005). A healthier cohort crossing a cleaner state transition might make the residual estimable,
so the 29% could understate the method in a favourable regime.

## Why it is not run
Two reasons, both decisive and recorded rather than worked around:

1. **No public venue (established by H4, `cbra_dataset_inventory`).** A re-test needs raw EEG +
   concurrent cardiac + an I⁺/I⁻ contrast + MR length in one corpus. The inventory found none
   exists besides I-CARE. The obvious "healthier cohort with a clean consciousness transition"
   (surgical emergence, VitalDB) supplies only a BIS index (not raw EEG) and is essentially pure
   I⁺ (no non-recovery contrast). So there is nowhere to run the re-test.

2. **Rescue-narrative risk (Paper 2 §3.3).** Moving the cohort *after* seeing the 29% is exactly
   the selection bias §3.3 was written to prevent: if the signal appears only in a cohort chosen
   after the negative, that is selection, not evidence. The re-test is therefore only legitimate
   **pre-registered, with the 60% bar fixed in advance, on a venue that also carries the I⁺/I⁻
   contrast** — which (per reason 1) does not currently exist.

## What would unblock it
A public (or accessible) cohort with concurrent raw EEG + cardiac, a genuine I⁺/I⁻ outcome
contrast, MR-length records, and a cleaner transition than post-arrest — none identified as of
2026-09. Until then the honest status is that the H3 mechanism (cardiac irreversibility degraded
in the sick cohort) is *plausible and literature-supported* but *not testable on available data*,
which reinforces Paper 2 §14.2's data-availability estimability condition rather than overturning
B1.

## Status
Pre-registered; blocked for want of a viable, contrast-carrying cohort (see H4). No result issued.
